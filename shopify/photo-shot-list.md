# Photo shot list — How it works page

Five slots, filled in the theme editor:
**Online Store → Themes → "HaloScalp — buy button update" → Customize → How it works page.**
Click the image area in each band → Select image → Upload. Each slot also accepts a
video file instead, if you have one.

Until a slot is filled it shows a grey Shopify placeholder, so fill all five before
publishing the theme.

---

## The single most important rule

**Shoot all five in one sitting.** Same person, same outfit, same room, same light,
same day. Five photos that obviously belong together look like a brand. Five photos
from five different places look like a dropshipper who grabbed whatever was on the
supplier's site — and that is exactly what the store is trying not to look like.

Twenty minutes with a phone will do it.

---

## Slot 1 — Hero (band 1, "Wear it. Get on with your day.")

A real person wearing the cap, mid-shot from about the waist up, doing something
ordinary — pouring coffee, reaching for a jacket. Looking away from the camera, not
smiling into the lens. Daylight from a window to the side.

The point of this shot is "this is a cap, and nobody would look twice". Not a
product shot.

## Slot 2 — The light (band 2, "What the light actually does")

Close-up of the inside of the cap with the LEDs lit, filling the frame. This is the
proof shot and the only one that really has to be good. Shoot it in a dimmer corner
of the same room so the red reads properly, but not in the dark — a pitch-black
background looks sinister and cheap.

Get in close. If your phone has a macro or 2x lens, use it.

## Slot 3 — Step 01, Charge

The cap on a clean counter or desk with the cable plugged in. Tidy the background
first — one mug is fine, a pile of post is not. Shoot slightly from above.

## Slot 4 — Step 02, Put it on, start low

Hands pulling the cap on, or a thumb on the controller. Show the shell flexing —
that is what sells "soft, not a rigid helmet". A tight crop on hands and cap works
better here than a full portrait.

## Slot 5 — Step 03, Carry on

The same person wearing it and clearly doing something else — laptop, washing up,
dog lead in hand. The red glow should be just visible at the brim. This is the shot
that carries the whole argument of the page, so give it the most attempts.

## Practical

- **Daylight.** Window to the side, never behind the subject, never overhead ceiling
  light — it goes yellow and flat.
- **Wipe the lens** before you start. It is always smeared.
- **Step closer rather than zooming.** Digital zoom destroys detail.
- **Shoot each one ten times** and pick later. Storage is free, reshoots are not.
- **No filters.** Straight out of the phone, maybe a small exposure nudge.
- **1600px wide minimum**, landscape or 4:5. Shopify handles the resizing.
- Do not mix in the supplier's banner images here. They have text baked into them
  and different lighting, and they are the single thing that most makes a store read
  as dropshipped.

## One thing to avoid

**No before-and-after photos.** They are a health claim, they are regulated, and the
store's own copy says plainly that results vary and are not guaranteed. A
before-and-after would contradict that on the same site and hand a regulator an easy
case. Show the product being used, never a result.
