Shipping policy

Where we ship

We ship to 28 countries: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom and the United States.

Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed at the weekend or on a public holiday are processed on the next business day.

Delivery estimates

After dispatch, expect approximately 7–18 business days across Europe and the United Kingdom, and 10–20 business days to North America, Asia, Australasia and the Middle East.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Customs processing, carrier backlogs and busy retail periods can add time. If your order is materially outside the window above, contact us and we will investigate on your behalf.

Shipping costs

Your exact delivery cost is calculated and displayed at checkout before payment. Standard domestic delivery is €6.00 and free on orders of €65 or more; domestic express delivery is €9.00. Delivery to all other countries is a flat €16.00.

Tracking

You will receive an order confirmation email when your order is placed, and a dispatch email containing tracking details once it ships.

Customs, duties and import taxes

Some orders ship from outside the European Union and the United Kingdom. Where import duties or taxes apply, they are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the price paid at checkout. Your carrier will contact you directly if a charge is due.

Incorrect addresses

Contact us as soon as possible if you have entered an incorrect address. If the order has not been dispatched we can usually correct it. Once the order is with the carrier we may be unable to make changes.

Lost or damaged deliveries

If your order arrives damaged, or tracking shows delivery but nothing has arrived, contact us. We will resolve it with either a replacement or a full refund. You will not be left out of pocket for a failure in transit.
